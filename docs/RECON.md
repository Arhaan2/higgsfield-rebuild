## Required row format for the completed inventory

Flow ID; user goal; entry URL; required inputs; meaningful choices; steps performed; observed success or stopping point; loading/error behavior; next action; evidence file(s); date/time; status (completed, observed, blocked, documentation_only); proposed keep/improve/omit decision and reason.

Use separate tabs/sessions safely; one owner controls authenticated browser interactions. Never confuse documentation with execution. Keep personal account or payment details out of public evidence. Preserve the original research files, and record new evidence separately so preliminary observations remain distinguishable.
